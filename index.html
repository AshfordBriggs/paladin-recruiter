You run the call. Paladin runs the file.

Six things happening on your screen while you're on the phone doing what you're great at

01

Pre-Call Dossier

Before the phone rings, Paladin builds the case file: LinkedIn parsed and enriched, resume cross-referenced against the job description, gaps pre-flagged, must-ask questions generated. You walk in knowing more than they think you know.

02

Live Transcript

The call streams to your dashboard in real time — speakers separated, sub-second latency. Every word, on the record, as it's said.

03

Dynamic Prompts

Follow-up questions generated from what the candidate just said, cross-referenced against their file. The question worth asking, on screen while there's still time to ask it.

04

Instant Context

Technical terms, certifications, and industry jargon defined on your screen the moment they come up. Zero pretending.

05

Trust Flags

Every claim checked against the resume, the LinkedIn profile, and the job description — live. When the story drifts from the file, you see it while you can still probe it.

06

The Record

Seconds after hang-up: narrative summary, strengths, concerns, red flags, action items — filed to the candidate's history automatically. No note-taking. No memory gaps.

⚑ Paladin advises. You decide what to ask. Nothing talks to the candidate but you.

Not a prototype. A phone company with a brain.

Built by Recruiters, for Recruiters

Paladin is an Ashford & Briggs product, born from years of real-world staffing experience.

We built Paladin because we lived the problem. As recruiters, we were drowning in research, notes, and follow-ups — and walking into calls where the candidate had more technology on their side than we did.

So we built something that's actually on the call with you. Not an AI that talks to candidates. Not a recording that gets scored after the moment to ask the follow-up is gone. A counter-intelligence layer that works while the conversation is happening — because that's when it matters.

Whether you're an independent recruiter or part of a staffing agency, Paladin fits the way you already work: your phone, your voice, your relationships. Just better informed.
