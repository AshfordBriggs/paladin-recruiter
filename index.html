Features

Everything you need to show up prepared and stay organized — without the busywork

Candidate Briefs

Before every call, Paladin prepares a brief — resume highlights, role fit, talking points, and potential concerns — so you sound like you've done your homework, because you have.

Draft Assist

Paladin drafts personalized outreach and follow-up messages based on each candidate's background. You review, tweak, and send — your voice, your relationships.

Call Prep & Notes

Get a structured agenda before every call and auto-generated notes after. Paladin captures key details so you can stay present in the conversation instead of scrambling to type.

Cultural Fit Insights

Ashford & Briggs' proprietary cultural assessment gives you a clear read on candidate-to-company compatibility before you make the introduction — not after.

Pipeline at a Glance

See where every candidate stands across every role. Paladin keeps your pipeline organized and flags what needs your attention — no more digging through spreadsheets.

How It Works

Paladin fits into the way you already recruit — it just takes the busywork off your plate

01

Add Your Roles

Drop in your open reqs and client details. Paladin pulls together everything you need — job context, company culture notes, and ideal candidate profiles.

02

Paladin Preps You

For every candidate, Paladin builds a brief — background research, fit analysis, suggested talking points. You walk into every call informed and confident.

03

You Make the Call

You pick up the phone. You have the conversation. The candidate talks to a real person who knows their background and genuinely cares about the fit.

04

Paladin Handles the Follow-Through

After the call, Paladin captures your notes, queues up follow-ups, and keeps your pipeline organized — so nothing falls through the cracks.

05

You Close the Deal

You built the relationship. You understood the fit. You made the placement. Paladin just made sure you had time to do what you do best.

Built by Recruiters, for Recruiters

Paladin Recruiter is an Ashford & Briggs product — born from years of real-world staffing experience.

We built Paladin because we lived the pain. As recruiters ourselves at Ashford & Briggs, we were drowning in admin work — researching candidates, writing notes, chasing follow-ups — and it was stealing time from the part that actually matters: the conversation.

Paladin is the assistant we always wished we had. It does the prep work, keeps things organized, and surfaces the insights you need — so when you pick up the phone, you're ready. The human connection is the product. Paladin just makes sure you have time for it.

Whether you're an independent recruiter or part of a large staffing agency, Paladin fits into the way you already work and frees you up to do more of what you're great at.
