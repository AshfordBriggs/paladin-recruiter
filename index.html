Features

Paladin runs on the actual phone call, routing through your dedicated PBX to power every feature in real time

Candidate Briefs

Before every call, Paladin prepares a brief: resume highlights, role fit, talking points, and potential concerns. You sound like you've done your homework, because you have.

Draft Assist

Paladin drafts personalized outreach and follow-up messages based on each candidate's background. You review, tweak, and send. Your voice, your relationships.

Live Suggestions

While you're talking, Paladin listens through your dedicated PBX and delivers live suggestions straight to your screen. Follow-up questions. Objection responses. Talking points. Key details you'd otherwise have to look up mid-call.

Cultural Fit Insights

Ashford & Briggs' proprietary cultural assessment gives you a clear read on candidate-to-company compatibility before you make the introduction. Not after.

Pipeline at a Glance

See where every candidate stands across every role. Paladin keeps your pipeline organized and flags what needs your attention. No more digging through spreadsheets.

How It Works

Paladin runs on the call itself. Here's how your dedicated PBX powers every conversation

01

Add Your Roles

Drop in your open reqs and client details. Paladin pulls together everything you need: job context, company culture notes, and ideal candidate profiles.

02

Paladin Preps Your Brief

For every candidate, Paladin builds a brief: background research, fit analysis, suggested talking points. You walk into every call informed and confident.

03

Call Routes Through Your PBX

You dial out through your dedicated Paladin PBX. Your recruiter has the full power of an AI system behind them, but the candidate never knows. No latency. No robotic pauses. No feeling like they got the bot because they weren't worth a real person's time.

04

Live Suggestions Hit Your Screen

As the conversation unfolds, Paladin delivers live suggestions right to your screen. Follow-up questions to ask. Objection handlers. Candidate details and talking points. No tab-switching, no scrambling.

05

Follow-Through & Close

After the call, Paladin delivers a full summary, queues follow-ups, and keeps your pipeline organized. You built the relationship. Paladin made sure nothing fell through the cracks.

Built by Recruiters, for Recruiters

Paladin Recruiter is an Ashford & Briggs product, born from years of real-world staffing experience.

We built Paladin because we lived the pain. As recruiters ourselves at Ashford & Briggs, we were drowning in admin work. Researching candidates. Writing notes. Chasing follow-ups. It was stealing time from the part that actually matters: the conversation.

So we built something that's actually on the call with you. Paladin routes every conversation through your own dedicated PBX server and delivers live suggestions to your screen as you talk. Follow-up questions. Talking points. Objection handlers. Candidate details. Everything you need without breaking your flow. The AI listens so you can focus entirely on the human in front of you.

Whether you're an independent recruiter or part of a large staffing agency, Paladin fits into the way you already work and frees you up to do more of what you're great at.
