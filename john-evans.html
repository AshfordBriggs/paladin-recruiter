I build Paladin Recruiter, a realtime advisor that sits on a recruiter's phone call and briefs, transcribes, and files every conversation for the firm.

Ashford & Briggs is a Jacksonville, Florida company. I built the recruiter product and the live call console, and I run our sales myself: the demo is a real call on real phone lines, and you watch the file build itself. My co-founder Matt Barker built the telephony, the phone servers, and the mobile app.

If you run a staffing firm and want to see it on a live call, email me or request a demo. Thirty minutes, nothing staged.
