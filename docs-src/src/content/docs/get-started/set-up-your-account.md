The first time you sign in, Paladin asks for three things: the phone that
should ring when candidates call you, the name candidates see on caller ID,
and a password of your own. It takes about a minute.

:::note[Before you start]
You need the email address and temporary password from your invite. Have
your cell phone nearby.
:::

1. Sign in with the email address and temporary password from your invite.
   The welcome screen opens. Select **Let's go**.
2. Under **Your phone number**, enter the number of the phone you want to
   ring when candidates call you, usually your cell. If it isn't a US number,
   choose the country code first. Select **Next**.
3. Under **How candidates see you**, check your **Display name (caller ID)**.
   This is the name candidates see when you call them, and the name Paladin
   uses for you in call scripts. Check that your **Timezone** is right, then
   select **Next**.
4. Under **Set your password**, enter a new password of at least 8
   characters, then enter it again under **Confirm password**. Select
   **Next**.
5. Check the summary, then select **Start recruiting**.

## What happens next

Paladin opens your **Home** page. From now on, when someone calls your
Paladin number, your phone rings. You don't need a desk phone or a plugin.

You can change any of these settings later in your **Profile**.

## If something goes wrong

- **The Next button stays gray.** Something on that step is missing. You
  need a phone number on step 1 and a display name on step 2. On the password
  step, the password must be at least 8 characters and both entries must
  match. If they don't, you'll see **Passwords don't match**.
- **Your temporary password doesn't work.** Ask the person who sent your
  invite for a new one.
- **You get an error when you select Start recruiting.** Your answers are
  still there. Select **Back** to check each step, then try again. If it
  keeps failing, use the feedback link at the bottom of this page.
