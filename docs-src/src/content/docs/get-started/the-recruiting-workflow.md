Every role follows the same loop: set up the job, bring in candidates, screen
them on the phone, and send the best ones to the hiring manager. Paladin does
the research and the write-ups along the way. Each step below links to its
own page.

## 1. Set up the job

**[Create a job](../../jobs/create-a-job/)** with the title, the client, and
the job description or posting link. Select **Research & Process** and Paladin
researches the role and the company and builds the job profile it uses to
judge candidates.

Then **[add the hiring manager](../../jobs/add-a-hiring-manager/)**: the
person who will receive your reports.

## 2. Bring in candidates

**[Add a candidate](../../candidates/add-a-candidate/)** from a résumé, a
LinkedIn link, or your notes, and Paladin builds their profile. Have a stack
of résumés? **[Upload many résumés](../../candidates/upload-many-resumes/)**
at once.

## 3. Match candidates to the job

**[Add a candidate to a job](../../candidates/add-a-candidate-to-a-job/)**,
or let Paladin suggest who to look at from the job's page. For each pairing,
Paladin scores the fit and writes an analysis: strengths, gaps, red flags,
and a call script. **[Read the fit analysis](../../candidates/read-the-fit-analysis/)**
before you call.

## 4. Screen on the phone

**[Call a candidate](../../calls/call-a-candidate/)** from their page. Paladin
rings your phone and connects you. While you talk,
**[the Call Console](../../calls/the-call-console/)** shows the transcript and
suggests questions. Away from your desk, use the
**[Paladin Dialer](../../dialer/place-a-call-from-your-phone/)** on your phone.

## 5. Review the call

A few moments after you hang up, the summary, key points, and action items
are on the candidate's record. See
**[After the call](../../calls/after-the-call/)**, and
**[Find a past call](../../calls/find-a-past-call/)** when you need one
later.

## 6. Report to the hiring manager

**[Create a candidate report](../../reports/create-a-candidate-report/)** from
the call or from everything on file, set your verdict, and edit it until it
reads right. Then
**[send it to the hiring manager](../../reports/send-a-report/)**.

## 7. Keep the pipeline current

**[Move the candidate along](../../pipeline/move-a-candidate-along/)** as
things progress, and
**[archive candidates](../../pipeline/archive-a-candidate/)** you're done
with so your lists stay short. Archiving can be undone.

:::note
If your team recruits for its own company, Paladin says **Roles** instead of
jobs and **Divisions** instead of clients. The workflow is the same.
:::
