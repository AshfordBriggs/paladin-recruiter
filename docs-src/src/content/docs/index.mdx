Paladin Recruiter is where you keep your candidates and jobs, and where your
calls with candidates happen. Start a call from Paladin, and it prepares a
brief beforehand, listens while you talk, and files the summary on the
candidate's record afterwards.

## How it works

1. **Candidates and jobs live in Paladin.** Each candidate has a record with
   their résumé, notes, and every call you've had with them.
2. **You call from your own phone.** The Paladin Dialer places the call
   through your Paladin number, so it's transcribed and the candidate sees
   your business line.
3. **Paladin does the write-up.** While you talk, the Call Console shows the
   transcript and suggests questions. When you hang up, the summary goes onto
   the candidate's record, under the job you called about.

## Get started

1. **Set up your account.** Sign in with your invite and set the phone that
   rings, your caller ID name, and your password.
   [Set up your account](get-started/set-up-your-account/)
2. **Install the Paladin Dialer** on your phone.
   [Get the Dialer](https://paladinrecruiter.com/dialer)
3. **Make your first call.** Open a candidate and select **Call Candidate**.
   [Call a candidate](calls/call-a-candidate/)
