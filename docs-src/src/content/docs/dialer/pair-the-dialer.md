Connect the app once, with the same login you use for Paladin in your
browser. After that, every call you place from the app goes out through your
Paladin number.

:::note[Before you start]
- Your account needs a Paladin number. Open your **Profile** in Paladin: if
  it says **Not assigned**, ask your Paladin admin to assign one.
- The phone you're setting up must be one of your **Phone Numbers** in your
  **Profile**. Paladin only accepts Dialer calls from those numbers.
:::

1. Open **Paladin Dialer**. On the **Calls** tab, tap **Connect now**. (Or
   tap the gear at the top right.)
2. Under **Connect to Paladin Recruiter**, enter your **Dashboard address**:
   the address you sign in to Paladin at in your browser. You can paste the
   whole address from your browser; the app keeps only the part it needs.
3. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, the same ones you use for the
   dashboard. Your phone's password manager can fill them in.
4. Tap **Connect**. The app signs you in and takes you back to your calls.
5. Open the **Contacts** tab. When your phone asks for access to your
   contacts, allow it.
6. On Android only: the first time you place a call, Android asks to let
   Paladin Dialer make and manage phone calls. Allow it.

## What the app asks for, and why

- **Contacts** (iPhone and Android). The app lists your phone's contacts
  next to your candidates and teammates so you can call them through Paladin.
  On iPhone the prompt says "Paladin Dialer shows your contacts so you can
  call them through Paladin." Your contacts stay on the phone; they're never
  uploaded.
- **Phone calls** (Android only). Lets the app place the call for you with
  one tap. If you don't allow it, the app opens your phone's dialer with the
  number filled in, and you tap call yourself.

The app doesn't ask for your microphone or for notifications. The call
itself runs in your phone's own Phone app.

## What happens next

You don't need a PIN. The app gets one from Paladin when you connect and
keeps it on the phone. To check which account the phone is on, tap the gear.
It shows **Connected**, your name, your Paladin number, and the dashboard
address.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Sign-in failed."** The dashboard address, email or password is wrong.
  Check that you can sign in with them in your browser, then try again.
- **"Your account has no phone number assigned."** Ask your Paladin admin to
  assign you a Paladin number, then tap **Connect** again.
- **"This account can’t get a PIN automatically."** A **DISA PIN** field
  appears. Ask your Paladin admin for your PIN, enter it (4 to 8 digits), and
  tap **Connect**.
- **"Enter your dashboard address, email, and password."** One of the fields
  is empty.
