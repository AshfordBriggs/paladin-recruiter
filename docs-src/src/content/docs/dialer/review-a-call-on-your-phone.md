Every call that went through Paladin is on the **Calls** tab. Open one to
read the summary and transcript, and to record the outcome while it's fresh.
What you save here is saved in your dashboard too.

1. Open **Paladin Dialer**. It opens on **Calls**.
2. Tap the call. To find an older one, type a name or number under **Search
   Paladin calls**.
3. Read **Summary**, **Key points** and **Action items**. To read the whole
   conversation, tap **Transcript**.
4. Under **Outcome**, tap the one that fits: **Scheduled next**, **Forwarded
   to client**, **Callback requested**, **No answer** or **Rejected**.
5. Add anything worth remembering under **Notes**, then tap **Save
   outcome**. The button changes to **Saved**.
6. To see the candidate's profile, jobs and documents, tap their name
   (**Profile, opportunities and documents**).

## What happens next

The outcome shows under the call in your **Calls** list, here and in the
dashboard. Reports made for the call are listed under **Reports**; tap one to
read it.

If the caller isn't a candidate in Paladin yet, you see **Name this caller**
instead of their name. Type their name under **Full name**, or tap **Link to
phone contact** to use the name from your phone's contacts, then tap **Save
name**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"No summary yet."** The summary appears a minute or two after the call
  ends. Go back and open the call again.
- **"Couldn’t save the outcome."** Check your connection and tap **Save
  outcome** again.
- **"Sign-in expired."** Your dashboard password changed. Reconnect the app;
  see [Change Dialer settings](../dialer-settings/). Then save again.
- **Tapping a call dials it instead of opening it.** Paladin hasn't
  registered the call yet. Wait a minute, pull down on **Calls** to refresh,
  then tap it again.
