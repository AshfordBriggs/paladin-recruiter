Tap a name or number in the Paladin Dialer and the app places the call
through Paladin. The person you call sees your Paladin number, and Paladin
transcribes the call and files it in your dashboard.

:::note[Before you start]
The app must be [connected to your account](../pair-the-dialer/).
:::

1. Open **Paladin Dialer** and choose where to call from:
   - **Contacts**: type a name or number under **Search people and
     numbers**, then tap the person. Each row shows where it comes from:
     **Candidate**, **Team**, or **Phone** (your phone's contacts).
   - **Calls**: tap the phone icon next to a past call.
   - **Keypad**: enter the number, then tap **Call via Paladin**.
2. Your phone's Phone app takes over. On iPhone, confirm the call when iPhone
   asks. On Android the call starts on its own.
3. Wait a few seconds while Paladin connects the call. You may hear tones
   before it rings through.
4. Talk as usual. Hang up in the Phone app when you're done.

:::caution
Calls are transcribed, and Paladin doesn't announce that on the call yet.
Tell the person you're calling.
:::

## What happens next

About 15 seconds into the call, a bar appears at the bottom of the app:
**Call in progress**. Tap it to follow the call live. See
[See a call live on your phone](../see-a-call-live-on-your-phone/).

The call shows at the top of **Calls** right away. In the dashboard's
**Calls** list, calls from the Dialer are marked **mobile**. If the number
belongs to a candidate in Paladin, the call is filed on their record.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Invalid number."** The Dialer only calls US numbers. Check that the
  number has 10 digits, or 11 starting with 1.
- **"No number on file for this contact."** Tap the info icon next to the
  contact to check their numbers, or add one to the candidate in Paladin.
- **The call doesn't reach the person.** This phone needs to be one of the
  phones under **Phone Numbers** in your **Profile**. Add its number there,
  then call again.
- **The call isn't transcribed.** It was placed from your phone's own dialer
  or Contacts app instead of the Paladin Dialer. Hang up and call again from
  the Paladin Dialer.

More fixes: [Fix Dialer problems](../dialer-troubleshooting/).
