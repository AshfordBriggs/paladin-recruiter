While a call you placed from the Dialer is in progress, the app can show the
Call Console: the live transcript and the AI's suggestions, on the same
phone. Viewing it doesn't affect the call.

:::note[Before you start]
The call must be one you [placed from the Paladin Dialer](../place-a-call-from-your-phone/),
or a call to your Paladin number.
:::

1. During the call, switch from your Phone app back to **Paladin Dialer**.
   Use speaker or headphones so you can talk while you look.
2. Tap the **Call in progress** bar at the bottom of the screen
   (**Open console**). You can also tap **Call in progress** at the top of
   the **Calls** tab.
3. The live console opens, titled **Live** and the number you called. It
   starts on **Intelligence**. Switch to **Transcript** or **Prep** with the
   tabs at the top.
4. To have Paladin explain the last thing that was said, tap **Explain
   that** on **Intelligence**.
5. Hang up in your Phone app when you're done. The console has no hang-up
   button; leaving it doesn't end the call.

## What happens next

When the call ends, the bar goes away. The summary appears on the call a
minute or two later: open **Calls** and tap the call. See
[Review a call on your phone](../review-a-call-on-your-phone/).

## If something goes wrong

- **The Call in progress bar doesn't appear.** Paladin takes about 15
  seconds to register the call. Switch away from the app and back to check
  again. If it still doesn't appear, the call didn't go through Paladin, for
  example because it was placed from your phone's own dialer.
- **"Couldn’t load the call console."** Check your phone's connection, go
  back, and tap the bar again.
