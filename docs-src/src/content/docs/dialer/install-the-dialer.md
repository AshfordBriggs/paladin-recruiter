The Paladin Dialer is the app you call candidates from on your phone. Calls
you place from it run through Paladin, so they're transcribed and filed in
your dashboard. Installing takes a few minutes. The full install page, with
the Android download button, is at
[paladinrecruiter.com/dialer](https://paladinrecruiter.com/dialer).

:::note[Before you start]
You need your Paladin account set up, with your cell phone's number on it.
See [Set up your account](../../get-started/set-up-your-account/).
:::

## iPhone

The iPhone app installs through **TestFlight**, Apple's own app for apps on
their way to the App Store.

1. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store. It's free and made by Apple.
2. Send us the email address your Apple ID uses. We send you an invitation.
3. Open the invitation, tap **Accept**, then **Install**.

## Android

1. On your phone, open
   [paladinrecruiter.com/dialer](https://paladinrecruiter.com/dialer) and tap
   **Download for Android**. Then open the downloaded file.
2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

## What happens next

The app opens on the **Calls** tab and asks you to connect it to your
account. Continue with
[Connect the Dialer to your account](../pair-the-dialer/).

## If something goes wrong

- **The TestFlight invitation doesn't arrive.** It comes from "Ashford and
  Briggs LLC via TestFlight". Check your spam folder. If it isn't there, check
  that the email you sent us is the one your Apple ID uses.
- **TestFlight can't install the app.** TestFlight is signed in with a
  different Apple ID from the one we invited. Sign in to TestFlight with the
  invited Apple ID.
- **Android says the app isn't installed.** You have an older Paladin Dialer
  on the phone from before September 2026. Uninstall it, then open the
  downloaded file again.
