Add the hiring manager from the job they're hiring for. Paladin creates a
contact record and links it to the job. One hiring manager can own several
jobs, and you send them candidate reports from their page.

1. Open **Jobs** and select the job.
2. Scroll to the **Hiring manager** card and select **Link / Create**.
3. Search by name, title, email, or company. If the hiring manager is
   already in Paladin, select them. You're done.
4. If they aren't, select **Create new hiring manager**.
5. Enter their **Name**. Add their **Title**, **Email**, and **Phone** if
   you have them. **Company** is filled in from the job; change it if it's
   wrong.
6. Select **Save**.

You can also add a hiring manager without a job: open **Hiring Managers**
and select **Add Hiring Manager**. Link them to a job later from the job's
**Hiring manager** card.

## What happens next

The **Hiring manager** card on the job shows their name and contact
details. Select the name to open their page. To link someone else, select
**Change**. To remove the link, select **Unlink**.

The hiring manager's page lists their contact details, their jobs, the calls
on those jobs, documents, and notes. Any recruiter can edit the contact
details: select a value to change it.

To send them a candidate report, open their page and select **Send
report**, then choose the report. Paladin downloads the report and opens a
draft email to them in your email app. Attach the downloaded report and
send it.

## If something goes wrong

- **The Save button stays gray.** The hiring manager needs a **Name**.
- **Send report is gray.** It needs two things: an email address on the
  hiring manager's page, and at least one candidate report on one of their
  jobs. Add their email under **Contact**. The list only includes reports
  from your own calls, unless you're an admin.
- **The card shows a name and a Convert to record button.** Someone typed
  the hiring manager's details on this job before hiring managers were
  records. Select **Convert to record** to turn them into one.
- **The same person shows up twice in Hiring Managers.** Search before you
  create, and link the existing one.
