Add the job from the **Jobs** page. Give it a title and a job description,
and Paladin researches the role. You end up with a job page that call
scripts, fit scores, and candidate matching all work from.

:::note[Before you start]
Have the job description handy, as a file or as text you can paste. A link
to the public posting helps too.
:::

1. Open **Jobs** and select **Add Job**.
2. Enter the **Title**, for example "Diesel Mechanic".
3. Under **Job Provider (Company)**, choose the client the job is for. If
   they aren't in the list, choose **+ Add New Provider**, enter their
   **Name**, and select **Save**.
4. If the job is posted online, paste the link into **Job Post URL**.
5. Add the job description. Drag the file into the box, or click the box to
   pick a file. You can also paste the text into **Paste job description**.
6. Select **Research & Process**.

To add the job without starting research, select **Add** instead of
**Research & Process**. You can start research later from the job's page
with **Research Job**.

## What happens next

The job's page opens and the research starts. A progress panel shows what
Paladin is doing, under **Agent Activity**. You can stay and watch or come
back later. When it's done, the page shows the research, including the
**Job Analysis**.

If you attached a file, it appears under **Documents** on the job's page.
Paladin reads it in the background.

The job starts as **active** and shows on your **Jobs** page and on
**Home**, because you created it, so you own it. You can edit the title,
description, and other details on the job's page: select a value to change
it.

Next steps:
- Link the job's hiring manager, so you can send them candidate reports.
  See [Add a hiring manager](../add-a-hiring-manager/).
- To have Paladin suggest candidates from your database, scroll to **Who to
  research** and select **Find candidates**.
- When the job is on hold, filled, or closed, select the status next to the
  job's title and choose the new one.

## If something goes wrong

- **The Add and Research & Process buttons stay gray.** The job needs a
  **Title**. Enter one.
- **A message says the file "exceeds 10 MB limit."** Use a smaller file, or
  paste the text into **Paste job description** instead.
- **A message says the file "has unsupported type."** Paladin reads PDF,
  DOCX, TXT, CSV, MD, HTML, JPG, and PNG files. Save the description in one
  of those formats, or paste the text.
- **You can't find the job on the Jobs page.** You may be looking at
  another recruiter's job. Tick **Show all jobs** to see everyone's.
- **The research shows something wrong about the job.** Fix the job
  description or add a note on the job's page, then select
  **Re-Research Job**.

:::note
If your team recruits for its own company, Paladin says **Roles** instead
of jobs and **Division** instead of job provider. The steps are the same.
:::
