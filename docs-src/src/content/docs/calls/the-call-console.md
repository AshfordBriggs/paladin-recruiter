The Call Console shows a call while it happens: what's being said, what
Paladin makes of it, and the brief it prepared. You talk and hang up on your
phone. The console has no buttons to dial or hang up, and closing it never
ends the call.

:::note[Before you start]
The console opens when you [call a candidate](../../calls/call-a-candidate/).
If a call is already under way, select **Open Call Console** at the top of
the web app.
:::

1. Check the status at the top of the **Intelligence** panel. It shows
   **Waiting for audio** until the call connects, then **Live** once Paladin
   hears you talking.
2. Follow the conversation under **Live Transcript**. Each line shows who
   spoke and how far into the call it was.
3. Watch **Intelligence** for cards. Each card is one topic of the
   conversation: notes collect under its heading, and **Ask about** lists
   questions to ask next, newest on top.
4. To have Paladin explain what was just said, select **Explain that**. The
   answer appears as a card titled **You asked**.
5. On a call about a job, use **Script** for the opening, the questions and
   the close. Use **Gaps & Questions** for the gaps to probe and the
   questions you must ask, and tick them off as you go.
6. On a General Call, check **Details**. Names, numbers and dates collect
   there as they're said.
7. Hang up on your phone when you're done.

### Job call or General Call

| | Call about a job | General Call |
|---|---|---|
| Left panel | **Script** and **Gaps & Questions**, from the brief | **Details**, filled in as you talk |
| Intelligence | Topic cards and suggested questions | Topic cards, plus **We said we'd** (what you promised) and **They want** (what they asked for) |
| Scores | **Consistency**, **Specificity** and **Communication**, after the first couple of minutes | None |

The menu next to **Explain that** shows which kind of help is running:
**recruiter** for a job call, **summary** for a General Call. You can switch
it for this call, but a General Call has no brief for **Recruiter** to work
from.

:::note[On a phone]
On a narrow screen the console shows one panel at a time. Use the tabs at the
top: **Intelligence**, **Transcript**, and **Prep** (**Details** on a General
Call). The phone layout hides the status, the mode menu and **Explain that**.
Use a computer for those.
:::

## What happens next

When both of you have hung up, the status shows **Call ended**. Paladin
keeps listening for a few seconds to finish its notes, then shows **All
analysis captured.** You can close the tab.

Paladin then writes the call summary. See [After the call](../../calls/after-the-call/).

To write a report for the client from this call, select **Generate
Report** at the top. It opens in the web app. If the call is still going,
Paladin asks first, because the transcript isn't complete yet.

## If something goes wrong

- **The status stays on "Waiting for audio."** The call hasn't connected
  yet. Answer your phone when it rings. If it never rang, close the tab and
  launch the call again from the candidate's page.
- **Explain that is gray.** It works only while the call is on, and one
  question at a time. While Paladin is answering, the button reads **AI
  responding…**.
- **You see "Call intelligence is temporarily unavailable."** The cards have
  paused. Keep talking; the transcript keeps running, and the summary after
  the call doesn't depend on the cards.
- **Script and Gaps & Questions are empty.** The call was launched as a
  General Call, so there's no brief. Next time, choose the job under **What
  is this call about?**

:::note
If your team recruits for its own company, Paladin says **Roles** instead
of jobs. The console works the same way.
:::
