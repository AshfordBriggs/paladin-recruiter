**Brief**
: What Paladin prepares before a call from everything on file: résumé,
  LinkedIn, notes, and the job description. It appears in the Call Console
  under **Prep** and **Script**.

**Call Console**
: The screen that opens in a new tab when you launch a call. It shows the
  live transcript and the AI's suggestions.

**Display name (caller ID)**
: The name candidates see when you call them. Change it in your **Profile**.

**Fit score**
: A percentage showing how well a candidate matches a job's requirements,
  based on everything on file.

**General Call**
: A call that isn't about one specific job. Paladin still transcribes it and
  writes a summary.

**Hiring manager**
: The person at the client (or in your company) who owns a job and receives
  your candidate reports.

**Job** (or **Role**)
: A position you're recruiting for. Teams that recruit for their own company
  see **Roles** instead of jobs.

**Job provider** (or **Division**)
: The client company a job comes from. Teams that recruit for their own
  company see **Divisions**.

**Opportunity**
: One candidate being considered for one job. A candidate can have several
  opportunities, one for each job.

**Paladin Dialer**
: The phone app you use to place calls through Paladin.
  [Install it](/dialer).

**Paladin number**
: Your business line. Calls to it ring your own phone, and calls you place
  through the Paladin Dialer show it as the caller ID.
