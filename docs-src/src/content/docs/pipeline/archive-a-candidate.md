Archive a candidate you're done with. They disappear from candidate lists,
your Home tab and matching, but nothing is deleted. Their calls, texts,
documents and opportunities stay, and you can restore them any time.

:::note[Before you start]
The candidate must be yours, or you must be an admin.
:::

1. Open **Candidates** and select the candidate.
2. At the top of the page, select **Archive**.
3. Confirm. You see "Candidate archived".

## What happens next

The candidate's page now shows "Archived by" with your name and the date,
and the **Archive** button becomes **Restore**. The candidate no longer
shows in the **Candidates** list, on your **Home** tab, or when Paladin
looks for matching candidates for a job.

If an archived candidate calls or texts you, Paladin still matches them to
their record. They stay archived.

## Find and restore an archived candidate

1. Open **Candidates**.
2. Under **Status**, choose **Archived**. The newest archived are at the
   top.
3. Select **Restore** on the candidate's row, or open the candidate and
   select **Restore**.

The candidate comes back exactly as they were. To go back to the full list,
select **Show all candidates**.

:::note
There is no delete button for candidates. Archiving is how you take
someone out of view, because deleting would also remove the calls and
history other recruiters may rely on.
:::

## If something goes wrong

- **There's no Archive button.** The candidate belongs to another
  recruiter. Ask them or an admin to archive it.
- **You see "Could not archive the candidate" or "Could not restore the
  candidate".** Nothing changed. Refresh the page and try again.
