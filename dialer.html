Install Paladin Dialer

Every call you make from the app runs through Paladin and shows up in your dashboard.

Android

Open this page on your phone and tap the button. Android will ask once for permission to install an app from your browser.

Download for Android
  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If asked, allow installs from your browser, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
  4. Enter your Paladin dashboard address, email and password. Allow access to Contacts.

Already have an older Paladin Dialer on the phone from before September 2026? Uninstall it first, then install this one.

iPhone

On iPhone the app installs through TestFlight, Apple's own app for software that is on its way to the App Store. Every iPhone app goes through it before listing.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store (free, made by Apple).
  2. Send us the email address your Apple ID uses. We send you an invitation.
  3. Open the invitation, tap Accept, then Install.
  4. Open Paladin Dialer and enter your dashboard address, email and password. Allow access to Contacts.

Invitations come from "Ashford and Briggs LLC via TestFlight". Check spam if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

Your first call

Pick a contact in the app and call them as you normally would. The call goes out with your Paladin number as caller ID and appears in your dashboard with the transcript a few seconds after you hang up. Calls made from your phone's own dialer are not captured, so use the app for work calls.

Questions or trouble installing: jevans@ashfordbriggs.com. Paladin Dialer reads your contacts on the phone to place calls; it never uploads them.
